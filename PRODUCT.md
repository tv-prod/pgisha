# Product — פגישת המראה

## Register
brand

## Users
Israeli business owners and entrepreneurs aged 25–50 who are already posting video content (mainly Instagram) but not seeing conversions. They feel invisible despite effort. They respond to social proof, authority, and the promise of a concrete plan — not more theory.

## Product Purpose
Conversion landing page for "פגישת המראה" — a free 60-minute video strategy session with TV Production's founders. Goal: get the visitor to book the meeting. Page flow: quiz (qualification) → loading screen → VSL + full sales page.

## Brand Personality
Bold. Direct. Premium but never corporate. Commanding confidence with Israeli directness. "Your videos should work for you, not just look good."

## Anti-references
- Generic SaaS landing pages (bland gradients, centering everything, icon grids)
- Cheap video production ads (loud clashing colors, stock photos)
- Overcrowded corporate sites with too much copy

## Design Principles
- Results first: every element should move the visitor toward booking, not admire itself
- Authority through restraint: the brand is confident enough to not shout
- Story over spec: pain narrative before solution, always
- Mobile-first Israeli market: most traffic is phone users
